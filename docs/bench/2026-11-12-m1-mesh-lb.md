# P2C pick (M1, #443)

Criterion micro-benchmark `mesh_lb` of `mimic-core` ([SPEC MSH-46](../SPEC.md#1221-m1--mimicconfig-and-the-mesh-view-440)):
one `Endpoints::pick` (two distinct draws, the lower `(in_flight + 1) / weight`), over 3, 100 and 1000
endpoints, with every endpoint at full weight (`steady`) and with all but one in slow start
(`slow-start`). Budget (design §14, M1): at most 2 µs.

```
cargo bench -p mimic-core --bench mesh_lb
```

| Date | Machine | Load during the run | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-12 | Intel i7-8750H @ 2.20 GHz, 12 threads, rustc 1.99.1, release profile | **not quiet**: load average 6.4 to 8 (other builds and tests of other agents on the same machine) | see below |

| Endpoints | steady (95 % interval) | slow-start (95 % interval) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 25.2 to 26.0 ns | 40.6 to 42.1 ns |
| 100 | 25.3 to 26.2 ns | 36.8 to 38.2 ns |
| 1000 | 24.2 to 25.1 ns | 36.5 to 37.9 ns |

The pick is O(1) and about 50 times under the 2 µs budget even on a loaded machine, so the budget holds.
